How these work

  • Go at your own pace. Each lesson is split into numbered sections — use the buttons at the top to move between them, or the Continue button at the bottom of each one.
  • Your work is saved in this browser. Close the tab and come back later and it will be where you left off. It is not sent anywhere, and it will not follow you to a different computer.
  • Answer before you reveal. Several questions hide their explanation until you have committed to an answer. Guessing first and reading after is the point — that is where the learning happens.
  • Print your work at the end. The last section turns everything you typed, plus the lesson notes, into one printable summary for revision or to hand in.
  • Read-aloud. Every section has a 🔊 button for the whole page, and a small one on each block.
  • Best on a laptop or desktop. They work on a phone, but the diagrams get small.
